## Key Points for Judges

✅ **Each judge scores ONLY their assigned event**
✅ **All criteria must be scored (0 to max)**
✅ **App automatically calculates totals**
✅ **Scores submit immediately to live scoreboard**

## Quick Scoring Tips

1. **Shadow Boxing (95)**: Focus on technical form and combinations
2. **Punching Bag (100)**: Technique matters more than raw power
3. **Skipping (110)**: Continuity is key - avoid breaks
4. **Boxing Combat (100)**: Most comprehensive - 10 categories to evaluate
